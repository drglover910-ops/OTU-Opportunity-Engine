## CRM & Workflow Audit — $250

**Description:** Get a clear picture of how your GoHighLevel account handles leads, follow-up and booking. I will review the agreed areas, identify practical gaps, and deliver a prioritized improvement plan so you can choose what to fix first.

**Included:** One GoHighLevel location; up to two pipelines and three existing workflows; one lead-entry-to-booking journey; a written findings report; a prioritized action list; one 30-minute walkthrough; one clarification revision. Read-only review: no live workflow edits, sending, contact exports or bulk changes.

**Delivery:** Three business days after agreed access and discovery answers are received, subject to availability confirmed before purchase. Planned work allowance: up to three hours. Larger audits receive a separate quote.

**Not included:** Implementation, custom API work, data migration, copywriting campaigns, compliance certification or ongoing support. Client subscriptions and usage fees are separate.

**Questions:** Do you fix the issues? This package diagnoses and prioritizes; implementation is separately scoped. Do I need an existing account? Yes, an accessible GoHighLevel location and a stated business goal. Can you promise more sales? No revenue or conversion guarantee. What does a revision cover? Clarification of the original findings, not a new account or additional systems.

**Cover concept:** Readable workflow map, magnifying-glass icon, title “CRM & Workflow Audit”; navy/charcoal with a restrained green accent. No fabricated dashboard results.

## GoHighLevel CRM Cleanup — $350

**Description:** Organize an agreed part of your GoHighLevel CRM so your team can understand the pipeline and follow the next step. We will define the cleanup first, then review and test the agreed changes before handoff.

**Included:** One location; one pipeline with up to seven stages; up to ten agreed tags or custom-field definitions; review of up to 500 contact records for organization and duplicate flags; changes only to explicitly approved fields; test checklist; before/after configuration summary; one 30-minute handoff; one revision within the original scope. Back up affected settings/data before changes. Duplicate records are flagged for review, not automatically deleted or merged.

**Delivery:** Five business days after access, backup and scope approval; availability confirmed before purchase. Planned work allowance: up to five hours. Additional pipelines, records or locations require a quote.

**Not included:** Contact enrichment, bulk deletion/merging, full migration, new automations, external integrations, historical data repair or unlimited revisions. Client software and third-party fees are separate.

**Questions:** Will you delete my contacts? No automatic deletions or merges. Can we add another pipeline? It can be quoted separately. What counts as a revision? One consolidated request correcting the agreed setup; new requirements are separate. Is monthly support included? No; an optional support plan can be agreed after handoff.

**Cover concept:** A simple “before → organized pipeline” illustration using fictional sample data clearly labeled as a demonstration.

**Not included:** Custom APIs, new website/funnel builds, large migrations, telephone provisioning/registration, additional locations, AI voice agents, ad spend, deliverability guarantees or continuous campaign management. GoHighLevel subscriptions, SMS/email charges and external software are paid by the client.

**Questions:** Are SMS/email charges included? No. What if my channels are not ready? We identify prerequisites and agree the timeline before starting. Do you guarantee bookings? No; we deliver and test the agreed system. Can I add more workflows later? Yes, through a separate scope. Will it message real leads during testing? Testing uses approved sample leads; live activation requires approval.
