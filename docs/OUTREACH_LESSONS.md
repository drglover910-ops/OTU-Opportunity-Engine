# Observed outreach lessons — v0.5.3

The private Sent review found coaching, CRM/GoHighLevel, automation, and business outreach. Buffalo Stallions has three related threads with nine sent messages and eight non-automated replies, including meetings and a revised compensation offer. These are evidence of progress; they do not establish a completed hire or payment.

Apply these patterns to future drafts:

- Explain why the specific role or project fits Desmond's supported experience. For CRM work, describe relevant pipelines, workflows, lead management, calendars, forms, or operational processes rather than promising every technical service.
- Match the materials to the request. Refer only to documents actually prepared and attached. The prior history includes tailored resumes, cover letters, and requested additional materials.
- Ask about the client's current systems, priorities, scope, implementation plan, or timeline. Let those answers guide a realistic proposal.
- After a meeting, reference the discussion and the organization's goals. Continue the existing conversation rather than sending another first introduction.
- If someone asks for clarification about experience, answer the question directly using the career record. Do not inflate qualifications.
- Keep follow-ups warm and specific, with one clear next step. A positive response is not proof that this wording caused success.

The app stores short professional style examples and provides recent thread context to optional AI writing. This is drafting guidance, not model training. Source facts continue to come from the career library. Exact outcomes and commitments require confirmation.
