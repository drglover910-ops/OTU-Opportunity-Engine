# Your email conversations — v0.5.3

Open **Activity & replies** to see **Study my sent outreach** and the reviewed conversations. Buffalo Stallions appears first. Select **Read conversation** to see the original messages in your private app.

The study searches your Sent folder for Buffalo Stallions and career/contract outreach, then reads each matching conversation's messages. It does not scan every unrelated email or download email attachments. A reply, a meeting, an offer, and a paid result are different stages. The bot does not mark a job won or paid based only on positive language.

Each thread becomes its own tracked opportunity. Original emails stay in the private Hugging Face database and private backups. The repository receives a redacted review summary. Existing emails are not resent. Hourly reply checks run while the Space is awake after the review completes; a sleeping Space cannot monitor continuously.

Writing uses concise introductions tied to the actual opportunity, requested-document replies, and follow-ups that acknowledge prior contact. Short examples from your history help optional AI drafts use your tone. Generated resumes still use the career library for factual claims. An email offer does not automatically become a master-resume achievement.

Messages require approval before sending. Historical conversations require an explicit intentional follow-up confirmation as well, to prevent duplicate first-contact outreach.
