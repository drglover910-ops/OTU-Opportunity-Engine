Safari tools for your iPad

Use the account you are already signed into. These tools run only when you choose their bookmark. There is no extra browser-service subscription.

1. Add the page reader once

This sends the page title, link, and visible text to your private bot for review. It does not submit applications or create accounts.

  1. In Safari, bookmark this page and name it OTU · Save opportunity.
  2. Open your bookmarks, choose Edit, and select that bookmark.
  3. Replace its website address with the copied tool. Keep the javascript: at the beginning, then save.
  4. Open a platform listing and choose that bookmark. Allow Safari to open the bot if asked. Review the captured details before saving.

If the platform or Safari blocks the tool, use Find opportunities → Save a listing and paste its link and requirements. Do not bypass platform restrictions.

2. Add your profile filler

Prepare and save your platform profile first. This tool contains only your reviewed public profile fields. It never includes passwords, identity documents, or payment details. Keep the bookmark private.

Save another bookmark named OTU · Fill my profile using the same steps. Use it on the signup or profile-editing page. Review every field, then create or save the account yourself.

The filler does not tick agreements, invent answers, attach identity documents, or press Submit. Copy a fresh filler after changing your profile. Some custom fields may need manual entry.
